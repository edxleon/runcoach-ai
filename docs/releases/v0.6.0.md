Every small item of the roadmap: a plan that says in advance what to do on a day it is too much, reps by pace, your short days kept short, a second load signal, the morning in one command, and Garmin's own readiness and your body weight beside the app's numbers.

```bash
uv tool upgrade runspector      # or, without uv: pipx upgrade runspector
runspector serve
```

**The plan**
- Every hard session and long run comes with its easier option: "if tired: an easy run of 48 min instead" in the preview, as the workout's description on the watch, beside today's session in the readiness tool, and in the red-day swap. One rule makes it - a hard session 10 minutes shorter and easy, at least 30; the long run one of the week's easy runs - the same rule the calendar fit already used.
- Reps go to the watch as a pace band (± 5 s/km) once your pace has a source: a measured threshold, or Garmin's 5 km and 10 km predictions. Heart rate lags a minute behind a four-minute rep. Without a source the reps keep their heart-rate zone.
- A weekday you always keep short - Tuesday at 35 minutes while the other runs go an hour - stays that short in the plan and gets no quality session while another day can take it. Read from your history, shown on the You tab with its evidence.
- Seven alike, heavy days (Foster's monotony above 2.0 with a week at least as heavy as the four before) hold the next week: no step up. The Trend tab shows the number; the coach hears it only when it is high.
- A held week after a down week is no bigger than the last regular week (it could jump past it before).

**The morning**
- `runspector today` prints today's call in a few lines - GO / EASY / REST with its sentence, the signals behind it, today's planned session with its easier option. `--sync` syncs first. Exit 3 when there is nothing from today yet, so a cron and a notifier can tell "not synced" from "rest day".
- Coming back to the page after more than 30 minutes (`RUNSPECTOR_AUTOSYNC_MIN`) syncs first instead of only re-reading.

**Garmin's numbers beside the app's**
- Garmin's own morning Training Readiness on the Today tab, in the readiness tool and in `runspector today`, on watches that compute it (a fenix 6S does not). The call stays the app's; no rule reads Garmin's score.
- Body weight from Garmin's weigh-ins (kg; nothing else of the body composition is kept), under the VO2max chart and in the VO2max tool, with how much the weight alone moves a per-kilogram VO2max once it changed by a kilogram or more.
- One more call per sync for each (today's readiness, 56 days of weigh-ins in one range call).

**Tested**
- Four more synthetic athletes run sixteen weeks of the app: one who runs the same every day, one with a short Tuesday, one whose profile asks for five runs with three days off, one who says yes to the week three days late (through the real write path against a Garmin double). The simulator found the held-week jump above.
- Three new eval cases: Garmin says 88, the app says easy; too tired for the intervals; a VO2max rise that is the weight.
