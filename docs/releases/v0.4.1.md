Your week is yours to change. The You tab shows the week the planner goes by, filled from your history and kept up to date by it - and every value can now be set by you. A note in your own words tells the coach what the numbers cannot.

```bash
uv tool install --force git+https://github.com/edxleon/runcoach-ai@v0.4.1
runcoach serve
```

**Your week** (You tab)
- Runs per week, your running days, the long run, days you can't run, another sport - each says where it comes from: "from your history" or "set by you".
- Edit it with a tap. What you set wins until you hand it back with "use my history"; everything you leave open the history keeps updating.
- A day you can't run is never planned on. If it was your usual long-run day, the long run moves (the weekend first) and the proposal says why.
- A week asking for more days than your days off leave gets the free days, with a line saying so; on the You tab the runs a week you set cannot exceed them.
- A day off you add leaves your own running days; the tab, the coach and the proposal all name the week the planner actually plans.
- The history stays visible next to what you set: "set by you; your history says sat".

**For your coach**
- A free-text note (up to 600 characters): "gym Mon, Wed, Fri", "less time until December". The coach reads it before every card and week plan, and the MCP tool `get_athlete_profile` hands it to the chat.
- It is context for the coach, not a rule: today's decision, the spacing and the load rules stay the app's, and nothing goes on the watch without your yes. What the planner itself keeps to are the days above.

**Fields that look like the app**
- The question field, the route form and the card inputs share one style: the card's own surface, a hairline border, and a quiet accent ring on focus instead of the browser's white box.

**Fixed**
- A sync read only the newest 50 workouts, about 14 weeks with runs and gym side by side. It now pages back 26 weeks, so the habits of a runner back from a pause are no longer empty. Zone detail is still fetched for the last 35 days.
- A change from a rotating rhythm to fixed days read "before: " with nothing; it says "before: no fixed weekdays".
- `runcoach profile` checks the week the same way the You tab does: a long run on a day off is refused.
- A return week of six days was planned as five, without a word: the easy runs never tried Tuesday and Thursday. Every free day is tried now (the golden test changed in exactly those 336 easy-only weeks).
